### Description

When fire moves through a watershed, a narrow window opens — one that can accelerate riverscape restoration work that might otherwise take years to fund and permit.

This webinar explores how to turn wildfire into opportunity by leveraging emergency funding and streamlined regulatory processes to implement restoration projects at speed and scale.

We'll cover:
- how to prepare before fire strikes,
- move quickly from concept to plan and design,
- and secure funding and regulatory approvals in the critical weeks that follow.

Whether you're a practitioner, land manager, or agency partner, you'll leave with a practical playbook for moving fast on post-fire funding.
